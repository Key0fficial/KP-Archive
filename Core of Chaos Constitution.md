# Core of Chaos Constitution

**Core of Chaos Constitution** was founded in 2020 ahead of the 2030 French Crisis, they are the first military branch of KeyPOWER's. The agency was established following a supernatural encounter experienced by "Thy Doctor" during his 2019 expedition into the Almanac Forests.

## — Development

After its establishment, KeyPOWER struggled to keep up with the manpower costs of over 1,000 men. To attract volunteer soldiers, KeyPOWER created a slogan framing their starvation as a form of endurance training.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e225921f-a1e9-4588-9d2d-0321fc580715" width="100" align="right">

The C.C.C. was originally planned as a mercenary group by the KeyPOWER shareholders, but Thy Doctor disagreed. He insisted that the C.C.C., true to its name, should operate only to establish peace and tranquillity, rather than fighting in warfare. However, he also pointed out that the C.C.C. would have to "destroy" to maintain that "tranquillity." Consequently, the C.C.C. considered as **Private Military and Security Company (PMSC)**.

The very name of the Chaos Core Constitution signifies the branch's role as the central authority over global stability and the fundamental laws of the world. The **"Core"** represents its position as the heart of power and the center from which all chaos will be secured, **"Chaos"** identifies the volatile and anomalous forces that the group is mendated to govern and destroy, and the **'Constitution'** represents that the C.C.C. is the supreme law that will manage chaos at will and by all means. This legal and physical restructuring ensures that "peace and security" are not only maintained, but also woven into the fabric of life, making any manifestation of chaos impossible.

One year after its establishment, following issues regarding upkeep and the branch's purpose, KeyPOWER’s high-ranking officials held several meetings in 2021. They discussed creating a dedicated internal hierarchy for the C.C.C. so that KeyPOWER would not be overwhelmed managing a military branch while their primary focus remained on researching technology and weaponry. An agreement soon was reached to base the C.C.C. hierarchy on standard military ranks, with **Seigneur de l'Intérieur** as the supreme position.

## — C.C.C. Regiments

The C.C.C. have over **50** specialized regiments, each was made for soldiers whose passed the test and categorized from its capabilities after result of years of rigorous, high-level training. Many of these regiments have achieved infamouse status, particularly those who commanding massive spaceship fleets and those involved in the high-stakes KeyPOWER experiments of 2029.

​The most devastatingly powerful unit in the organization is the 25th Régiment de la C.C.C. Originally designated as the 2nd Régiment de la C.C.C., they were renumbered following their involvement in the 2021-era KeyPOWER biological experiment. The regiment was choosed for experiment because their excellent training resilts. These men after the experiments possess a physique very tough that they're valled **"Superbuff men"** due to their big muscles aftermath of the experiment. 

​Through the administration of the **I Vitus Superserum**—a breakthrough developed by Dr. Gilbert—their biology was fundamentally altered. These soldiers possess superhuman durability, capable of withstanding gravitational pressures twice of the Sun. While their movement speed remains within standard human parameters, their explosive power allows them to leap to the top of skyscrapers. Their physical strength is equally immense, enabling them to bend industrial superalloys and crush lead with ease.
