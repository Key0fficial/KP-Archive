 ## What is KeyPOWER?

**KeyPOWER** is a corporate company founded by Dr. ███ Rossomme at the turn of the millennium between 2000 and 2001, KeyPOWER began as a research company that focuses in researching technology, and biological weapons.

KeyPOWER is publicly recognized as a company that transcends the universe. Originally focused solely on investment profits, we evolved into a global powerhouse, forming branches to maximize our revenue.

During the 2030–2035 national crisis, we established two complex military mercenary groups to oppose the ██████ Government, ultimately utilizing our home nation as a pawn for the greater good and momentum for our influence over the geopolitics. 

Building on this momentum, we placing the nation’s entire economic infrastructure under our direct control. By centralizing amd stabilizing the nation's economy after the crisis, we have not only doubled our founding profits but have also gained the leverage needed to persuade other nations to fund our project and share technology. 

By decades, KeyPOWER doesn't just compete; we collaborate with world powers as an equal, expanding our influence through a web of high-level international partnerships.

With the resources of entire nations at our disposal, we have claimed dominion over the skies. Our engineers have developed a revolutionary reactor—a critical milestone in our ascent toward becoming a Type-III Civilization. This technology grants us energy capabilities previously thought impossible, ensuring that while our rivals may be many, we remain perpetually three steps ahead.

## Plan for the Future

The horizon of our ambition has moved far beyond Earth. With constellations, star systems, and entire galaxies now within our grasp, KeyPOWER has achieved total autonomy, relying exclusively on the resources of our colonized planets and severing all ties to the aid of other nations. We are no longer merely a company; we have become the architects of the future universe. 

As we ascend, we continue to revolutionize the cosmic order by developing biological cloning as a primary source of manpower for our military branches, leveraging quantum physics to engineer unparalleled weaponry, build multiple space-shipyards, and manifesting exotic, unknown materials harvested from the deepest reaches of the universe.
